Let's be direct: Avalara was built for larger businesses. If you're a small online seller — doing $50K, $100K, even $400K a year — you don't need enterprise sales tax software. You need something that actually fits your business.

This guide breaks down who Avalara is built for, why it's overkill for most small sellers, and which alternatives make more sense.

## Why Avalara Exists (And Who It's Actually For)

Avalara solves real problems — just not the problems most small sellers have.

**Where Avalara fits — larger businesses dealing with:**
- Selling into all 50 states with complex product taxability rules
- Managing thousands of transactions per day
- Cross-border international tax compliance
- Integration with enterprise ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite)
- Tax for software subscriptions, digital goods, and services with state-by-state variations
- Large teams that need audit trails, advanced reporting, and compliance staff

If you're a $10M/year company with a finance team and a CFO worried about a sales tax audit, Avalara makes sense.

If you're a $150K/year Shopify or WooCommerce store selling physical products to US customers, it's massive overkill.

### Sails — Best for Small Sellers

[Sails](https://sails.tax) is designed specifically for small online sellers. It imports orders from Shopify and WooCommerce (BigCommerce is in beta), checks your sales against every state's nexus rules, and starts completely free.

**Pricing:**
- **Free**: $0 — nexus monitoring, alerts, reminders and reports for 1 store connection, up to 50 orders/month
- **Starter**: $9/month — 2 stores, up to 500 orders/month
- **Pro**: $29/month — 3 stores, up to 5,000 orders/month, API access
- **Enterprise**: $79/month — unlimited

For a seller doing a few hundred orders a month, Starter at $9 handles it. For a seller doing 2,000+ orders, Pro at $29 covers up to 5,000 orders a month.

**What you get:** Nexus monitoring with email threshold alerts, a filing deadline calendar with email reminders, sales-by-state reports with CSV export, and a tax calculator for rate estimates.

**What's missing:** Sails doesn't file returns or remit tax, and its calculator uses state and average local rates (estimates), not exact address-level rates. If you need automated filing, TaxJar has the edge.

### TaxJar — Best Mid-Market Option

TaxJar is owned by Stripe (which acquired it in 2021) and is a well-respected tool in the ecommerce tax space. Their AutoFile feature files your returns for you, for a per-return fee.

**Pricing** (as of September 2026 — no free plan, 30-day free trial):
- **Starter**: from $39/month (up to 200 orders/month); AutoFile $50 per return
- **Professional**: from $99/month; AutoFile $55 per return

TaxJar is the sweet spot for sellers who want checkout calculation and filing handled in one place. But for sellers who are just getting started or only need nexus tracking, $39–$99/month is a lot.

### Quaderno — Best for Digital Products

If you sell software, digital downloads, SaaS subscriptions, or online courses, Quaderno is worth a look. Digital goods taxation has different rules by state and country, so check Quaderno's current features and pricing before you commit.

### When You Actually Might Need Avalara

We're not here to tell you Avalara is bad — it's genuinely the right tool for some businesses. Consider it if:

- You're doing more than $1M/year in revenue
- You sell across multiple countries and need international VAT/GST compliance
- You have complex product taxability questions (industrial equipment, food with varying tax rules, etc.)
- You need deep ERP integration (SAP, Oracle, etc.)
- You have compliance staff who need enterprise-grade audit trails

For everyone else: you're paying for a fighter jet when you need a reliable car.

## How to Choose

Ask yourself three questions:

**1. How many orders do you process per month?**
Under 500/month → Sails Starter ($9/mo) or TaxJar Starter (from $39/mo as of September 2026, up to 200 orders/mo)
500–5,000/month → Sails Pro ($29/mo) or TaxJar Professional (from $99/mo as of September 2026)
5,000+/month → Sails Enterprise ($79/mo), TaxJar Professional, or evaluate Avalara

**2. Do you need automatic return filing?**
Yes → TaxJar with AutoFile is your best bet
No / Not yet → Sails is cheaper and handles nexus monitoring, deadline reminders and reports

**3. Do you sell digital goods internationally?**
Yes → Quaderno
No → Sails or TaxJar
