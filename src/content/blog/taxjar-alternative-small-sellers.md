TaxJar used to be the go-to recommendation for small e-commerce sellers who needed sales tax compliance without the Avalara price tag. But things have changed — and if you're shopping for a TaxJar alternative, you're not alone.

Here's what's going on, what to look for in a replacement, and why small sellers increasingly have better options.

## What Happened to TaxJar?

In 2021, Stripe acquired TaxJar, and it has been part of Stripe ever since.

**What TaxJar costs today (as of September 2026):**

- There's no free plan — just a 30-day free trial
- **Starter** starts at **$39/month** (up to 200 orders a month); **Professional** starts at **$99/month**
- Filing through AutoFile costs extra: $50 per return on Starter, $55 per return on Professional

For a Shopify seller doing $80K/year or a WooCommerce store with $300K in revenue, $39–$99/month ($468–$1,188/year) is a significant expense if what you mainly need is to know where you have nexus.

## What You Actually Need as a Small Seller

Before comparing alternatives, it helps to define what sales tax compliance actually requires for a small online seller:

**The basics:**
- Know which states you have nexus in (physical and economic)
- Track when you approach or cross economic nexus thresholds
- Know what you owe in each state per filing period
- Get reminded when deadlines are coming

**Nice to have:**
- Direct integration with your sales platform (Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.)
- Multi-platform aggregation if you sell on more than one channel
- Filing-ready reports you can hand to your accountant

**What most small sellers don't need:**
- Real-time tax calculation at checkout (Shopify has it built in; WooCommerce needs a plugin)
- Automated filing in every state (many small sellers file quarterly — confirm the frequency each state assigns you)
- Enterprise audit trails and compliance documentation
- Cross-border international tax management

The problem with TaxJar's current pricing is that you may be paying for features you don't use if you only need the basics.

## What to Look for in a TaxJar Alternative

### 1. Honest pricing for your revenue level

If you're doing under $1M/year in sales, you should not be paying $99–$200/month for sales tax software. Look for tools with transparent, low-cost entry tiers that match your actual revenue.

As of September 2026, TaxJar starts at $39/month (Starter, up to 200 orders/month) or $99/month (Professional), with AutoFile billed per return on top. If you mainly need nexus monitoring, that's more than you need to spend.

### 2. Platform integrations that actually work

The alternative you pick should have a direct, native integration with your store platform — not just a CSV export workflow. If you sell on Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce, the tool should connect and sync automatically.

### 3. Multi-platform support

Many small sellers sell on more than one channel: Shopify plus Etsy, or WooCommerce plus Amazon. Your compliance tool needs to aggregate all of that, not just one platform. Tracking nexus from only half your sales gives you a false sense of security.

### 4. Nexus monitoring, not just calculation

Real-time tax calculation at checkout is table stakes (and your platform already does it). What you really need is **nexus monitoring** — a tool that watches your aggregate sales in each state and tells you when you're approaching a threshold. That's what prevents surprise tax bills.

### 5. Simple, clear reporting

Your accountant or tax preparer will need to see your tax collected by state. The alternative you choose should generate this report easily, without requiring you to become a tax software expert.
