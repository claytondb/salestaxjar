Understanding economic nexus thresholds is crucial for any online seller. Since the 2018 *South Dakota v. Wayfair* Supreme Court decision, states can require you to collect sales tax even without a physical presence — if you exceed their economic nexus threshold.

This comprehensive guide covers every state's current thresholds, updated for 2026. Rules change often, so always verify with the state before you register.

### States with Higher Thresholds

A few states have higher thresholds, giving small sellers more runway:

| State | Threshold |
|-------|-----------|
| **California** | $500,000 gross revenue |
| **Texas** | $500,000 gross revenue |
| **Alabama** | $250,000 in sales |
| **Mississippi** | More than $250,000 in sales |

### States Requiring Both Sales AND Transaction Count

Two states require you to meet **both** a dollar amount **and** a transaction count:

| State | Sales | Transactions |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **New York** | $500,000 | AND more than 100 transactions |
| **Connecticut** | $100,000 | AND 200 transactions |

**Note:** Unlike the "or 200 transactions" states above, New York and Connecticut require you to meet *both* thresholds, not just one.

### States with No Sales Tax

These five states have no state-level sales tax, so state economic nexus doesn't apply:

- **Alaska** (but many local jurisdictions collect sales tax, often through the Alaska Remote Seller Sales Tax Commission, which uses a $100,000 threshold)
- **Delaware**
- **Montana**
- **New Hampshire**
- **Oregon**

## State-by-State Breakdown

### Alabama
- **Threshold:** $250,000 in sales (prior calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** October 1, 2018
- **Notes:** Simplified Sellers Use Tax program available. Remote sellers can collect a flat 8% rate.

### Arizona
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales (prior or current calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** October 1, 2019
- **Notes:** Transaction privilege tax (TPT) — unique structure where seller is taxed, not buyer.

### Arkansas
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales OR 200 transactions (prior or current calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** July 1, 2019
- **Notes:** Standard collection applies. Register through the Arkansas Department of Finance.

### California
- **Threshold:** $500,000 in sales (prior or current calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** April 1, 2019
- **Notes:** Higher threshold benefits smaller sellers. Combined rates can exceed 10% in some areas.

### Colorado
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales (prior or current calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** December 1, 2018
- **Notes:** Complex due to home-rule cities. Many localities administer their own sales tax.

### Connecticut
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales AND 200 transactions (12 months ending September 30)
- **Effective Date:** December 1, 2018
- **Notes:** Must meet BOTH thresholds to trigger nexus.

### Florida
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales (prior calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** July 1, 2021
- **Notes:** Relatively late adopter. No state income tax makes sales tax revenue important.

### Georgia
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales OR 200 transactions (prior or current calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2019
- **Notes:** Marketplace facilitator law also in effect.

### Hawaii
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales OR 200 transactions (prior or current calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** July 1, 2018
- **Notes:** Hawaii uses a General Excise Tax (GET), not traditional sales tax. Applied to gross income.

### Idaho
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales (prior or current calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** June 1, 2019
- **Notes:** Standard implementation with streamlined filing.

### Illinois
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales (prior 12 months)
- **Effective Date:** October 1, 2018
- **Notes:** Transaction threshold removed effective January 1, 2026. Home rule municipalities can add additional taxes.

### Indiana
- **Threshold:** $100,000 in sales (prior or current calendar year)
- **Effective Date:** October 1, 2018
- **Notes:** Transaction threshold removed effective January 1, 2024. Member of Streamlined Sales Tax.

### Iowa

### Kansas

### Kentucky

### Louisiana

### Maine

### Massachusetts

### Michigan

### Minnesota

### Mississippi

### Nebraska

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What sales count toward the threshold?

Generally, only sales delivered to customers in that state count. Depending on the state, this can include:
- Taxable sales
- Non-taxable sales (like wholesale)
- Exempt sales

Most states look at gross sales, not net sales. Returns and refunds may or may not reduce your count, depending on the state.

### Are marketplace sales included?

It depends on the state. Many states (including California, New York, Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Washington):
- **Include** marketplace sales for determining if you have nexus
- **Exclude** them from your direct collection responsibility (since the marketplace collects)

Others, including Illinois, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Arizona, Tennessee and Virginia, leave marketplace sales out of your threshold.

This means selling $90,000 directly + $20,000 on Amazon could trigger nexus in a $100,000 threshold state that counts marketplace sales, even though Amazon remits the tax on their portion.

### When do I need to start collecting?

It varies by state. Some expect you to register and start collecting as soon as you cross the threshold, others from the first day of the next month, and states that look at the previous calendar year (like Florida and Pennsylvania) from the next calendar year. Don't wait — register promptly to avoid penalties.

### What's the penalty for not collecting?

Penalties vary by state but can include:
- Back taxes owed on uncollected sales
- Interest (typically 10-12% annually)
- Penalties (often 10-25% of tax owed)
- In extreme cases, personal liability

### Do thresholds reset each year?

Usually. Most states calculate thresholds based on the current or previous calendar year, though some use a rolling 12-month or four-quarter period (see each state above). If you exceeded the threshold last year, you typically have nexus for the entire current year.
